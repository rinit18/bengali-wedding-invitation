## Visual system

The live desktop opening was observed as a warm ivory/cream paper illustration with marigold flowers, mango leaves, a central sindoor-red and gold seal, Bengali display lettering, and ceremonial objects arranged symmetrically. The rest of the page is a sequence of full-screen/scroll-pinned scenes: the bride portrait, groom portrait and flower curtain, story, two families, event sequence, printed invitation, schedule, and closing image. Motion uses scroll-linked transforms, a page progress rail made from alternating footprints, floating butterfly and petal effects, animated flower lights, curtains, seal rings, and a live countdown.
